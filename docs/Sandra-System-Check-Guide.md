# 🛠️ **Casey Cranes Office Computer Helper**  
## _Sandra's Easy Guide to the Weekly System Check_

---

### 🧰 What It Is  
This is a small tool installed by Benno that quietly helps your computer stay healthy. It checks the internet, Outlook email, and file clutter — and gives a simple report once a week.

---

### ✅ What It Does  
- Verifies your internet and Outlook email connections  
- Checks that our website address is working correctly  
- Scans for duplicate or messy files on your computer  
- Opens a report in Notepad to show what was checked

---

### 🖱️ How to Use It  
1. Find the desktop icon labeled: **🛠️ Casey System Check**  
2. Double-click it  
3. Wait a few seconds — you'll hear a beep when it's finished  
4. A report will open in Notepad showing what's been checked  
5. You don't need to do anything else. Benno will see the report and help if needed

---

### 📁 Where the Reports Are Saved  
All results are saved safely in this folder:  
`C:\SystemReports`

Each report includes the date and time for easy tracking.

---

### 🕓 When It Runs  
- Automatically every Sunday morning  
- You can also click the icon any time things feel slow or confusing

---

### 🔐 Is It Safe?

- ✅ Benno built it to help keep your emails, files, and system working  
- ✅ No one can access this information except you and Benno  
- ✅ It's here to protect your business and make your work easier
