## FILMING STRATEGY

### Equipment You Need:
✅ **Smartphone** (iPhone or Android) - already have it!
✅ **Gimbal stabilizer** ($100-300) - for smooth footage
✅ **Drone** (if you don't have one) - for aerial shots
✅ **Lapel mic** ($30-50) - for interviews
✅ **Tripod** ($20-50) - for static shots

**You don't need expensive equipment. Your phone is enough to start!**

---

### Filming Tips:

**DO**:
✅ Film in landscape for YouTube/Facebook
✅ Film in vertical for TikTok/Reels/Stories
✅ Film during golden hour (sunrise/sunset) when possible
✅ Get multiple angles of the same action
✅ Capture audio of equipment (satisfying sounds!)
✅ Film operator faces (builds connection)
✅ Get before/after shots
✅ Film safety protocols

**DON'T**:
❌ Film into the sun (unless intentional silhouette)
❌ Forget to get client permission
❌ Film shaky handheld (use gimbal or tripod)
❌ Ignore audio quality
❌ Forget wide establishing shots

---

### What to Film on Every Job:

**Arrival** (30 seconds):
- Crane arriving at site
- Operator getting out
- Professional setup

**Setup** (1 minute):
- Outriggers deploying
- Boom extending
- Safety checks

**Action** (2-3 minutes):
- The actual lift
- Multiple angles
- Close-ups and wide shots
- Operator in cab

**Completion** (30 seconds):
- Successful placement
- Happy client (if possible)
- Equipment packed up

**Total filming time**: 5-10 minutes per job
**Usable content**: 10-15 short videos!
