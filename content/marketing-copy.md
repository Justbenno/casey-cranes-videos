## Brand Voice Guidelines

**Tone:** Professional, confident, reliable, approachable

**Style:**
- Clear and direct communication
- Focus on safety and quality
- Emphasize experience and expertise
- Friendly but professional
- Solution-oriented

**Avoid:**
- Overly technical jargon (unless B2B context)
- Aggressive sales language
- Negative comparisons to competitors
- Exaggeration or unrealistic claims
