## Focus on What You Deliver, Not What Others Lack

**Created by**: Manus AI  
**Date**: October 26, 2025  
**Purpose**: Reframe all messaging to focus on positive outcomes and strengths

## THE PRINCIPLE

**DON'T talk about**: Problems, failures, breakdowns, unreliability  
**DO talk about**: Success, reliability, consistency, excellence, trust

**Why**: You want people to associate Casey Crane Hire with positive outcomes, not negative scenarios (even if you're the solution).
