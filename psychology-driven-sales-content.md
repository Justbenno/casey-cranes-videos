## PSYCHOLOGICAL PRINCIPLES USED:

1. **Loss Aversion**: People fear losing more than they desire gaining. Emphasize what they lose by choosing wrong.

2. **Social Proof**: "500+ builders can't be wrong" - humans follow the crowd.

3. **Authority**: Certifications, experience, statistics build trust.

4. **Scarcity**: Limited slots create urgency and perceived value.

5. **Reciprocity**: Give value first (free checklist, free site assessment) and they'll want to give back.

6. **Fear Then Relief**: Present the problem (delays, costs, stress) then offer the solution (Casey Crane Hire).

7. **Specificity**: "1000+ projects" is more believable than "lots of projects."

8. **Contrast Principle**: Make competitors look risky by comparison.

9. **Commitment & Consistency**: Once they request a quote, they're mentally committed to hiring someone.

10. **Anchoring**: Mention the cost of delays ($47,000) before your price to make your price seem small.
