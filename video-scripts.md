## PRODUCTION NOTES & BEST PRACTICES

### For TikTok/Instagram Reels:
- **First 1-2 seconds are CRITICAL** - must stop the scroll
- Use trending sounds when possible
- Text overlays are essential (most watch without sound)
- Fast cuts every 2-3 seconds
- End with clear CTA and "Link in bio"
- Use relevant hashtags in caption

### For YouTube Ads:
- Hook in first 5 seconds (before skip button)
- Can be longer and more detailed
- Include phone number and website
- Professional voiceover recommended
- End card with clear CTA

### For Facebook:
- Captions/subtitles essential (autoplay is muted)
- Can be slightly longer than Instagram
- Include website link in post
- Boost/promote for best results

## TESTING & OPTIMIZATION

### A/B Test These Variables:
1. Hook style (fear vs. inspiration vs. data)
2. Video length (10s vs. 15s vs. 30s)
3. Music choice (epic vs. upbeat vs. tension)
4. CTA wording
5. With/without voiceover

### Success Metrics to Track:
- **View-through rate** (how many watch to end)
- **Click-through rate** (link clicks)
- **Engagement rate** (likes, comments, shares)
- **Conversion rate** (quote requests from video)
- **Cost per lead** (if running paid ads)

### Optimization Tips:
- Post at peak times (7-9 AM, 12-1 PM, 5-7 PM weekdays)
- Reply to all comments within 2 hours
- Pin best comment to top
- Share to Stories 24 hours later
- Repost top performers monthly
